[h1]ASW Find, Fix and Prosecute (v0.1.1 Beta)[/h1]

Pick a pattern, click the map, and your aircraft and helicopters lay out and drop a full sonobuoy field for you, efficiently and without gaps. Share the job with other aircraft and the mod splits the buoys between them. When a buoy detects a submarine, your aircraft fix it with a tight DICASS cluster and can prosecute it.

The mod works out buoy positions from the detection range of the buoys aboard and orders the drops so the aircraft flies an efficient track. It queues them using the game's own sonobuoy drop order.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[b]Patterns[/b]
[list]
[*]Grid at datum. The circle you drag sets its size, and a second click rotates it.
[*]Grid along a fleet route, with a choice of width.
[*]Corridor along a fleet route, 20-100 nm wide, perimeter first.
[*]Ring with infill.
[*]Straight line.
[/list]
[b]Smart layout[/b]
[list]
[*]Spacing comes from each buoy type's detection range.
[*]It skips buoys already in the water or already queued.
[*]Buoys over land are removed.
[*]Drop order is optimised so the aircraft does not fly over a later drop point on the way to an earlier one.
[*]Mixed buoy loads are handled.
[/list]
[b]Coordination[/b]
[list]
[*]Sharing puts the aircraft in one formation. Fixed-wing aircraft and helicopters can work one pattern together.
[*]Buoys are split by each aircraft's position, speed and buoy count, then rebalanced.
[*]Adding or removing an aircraft mid-pattern recomputes the layout and keeps buoys already placed.
[/list]
[b]Contact response[/b]
[list]
[*]Triggered by any sonobuoy-detected submarine contact, hostile or unknown.
[*]The closest idle aircraft carrying at least 3 DICASS drops a tight cluster on the contact's last known position.
[*]It assesses the fix, and attacks automatically only if the contact is hostile and the fix is good.
[*]It then resumes its interrupted pattern.
[/list]
[b]Convenience[/b]
[list]
[*]"ASW Pattern" is on the level-1 right-click menu.
[*]An "ASW" bottom-bar button: left-click repeats the last pattern, and the menu is available from there too.
[*]"Resume buoy deployment" restores a wiped queue.
[*]Map markers show queued buoys.
[*]A detailed log (Sea Power\BepInEx\ASWAutoHunt.log) records every planned and dropped buoy, and every contact response.
[/list]
