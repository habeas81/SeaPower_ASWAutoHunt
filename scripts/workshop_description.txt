[h1]ASW Find, Fix and Prosecute (v0.1.3 Beta)[/h1]

Pick a pattern, click the map, and your aircraft and helicopters lay out and drop a full sonobuoy field for you, efficiently and without gaps. Share the job with other aircraft and the mod splits the buoys between them. When a buoy detects a submarine, your aircraft fix it with DICASS or a dipping sonar and, on Weapons Free, prosecute it.

The mod works out buoy positions from the detection range of the buoys aboard and orders the drops so the aircraft flies an efficient track. It queues them using the game's own sonobuoy drop order.

[b]REQUIRES: Sea Power 0.9.0+ and Anchor Chain 1.2.0[/b] (Workshop item 3380210757).

[*][b]Fixed:[/b] "Cancel ASW pattern" now fully releases the aircraft (helicopters could ignore return-to-base or other orders afterwards).
[*][b]Fixed:[/b] an aircraft leaving a shared pattern now re-plans the others; sharing no longer drops a second buoy on a point just dropped.
[*][b]Fixed:[/b] an interrupted pattern no longer loses undropped buoys when it resumes.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[b]Patterns[/b]
[list]
[*]Grid at datum. The circle you drag sets its size, and a second click rotates it.
[*]Grid along a fleet route, with a choice of width.
[*]Corridor along a fleet route, 20-100 nm wide and twice as long, perimeter first.
[*]Ring with infill.
[*]Straight line.
[/list]
[b]Smart layout[/b]
[list]
[*]Spacing comes from each buoy type's detection range, so neighbouring circles only just overlap.
[*]It skips buoys already in the water or already queued.
[*]Buoys over land are removed.
[*]Drop order is optimised so the aircraft does not fly over a later drop point on the way to an earlier one.
[*]Mixed buoy loads are handled.
[*]Points no aircraft has buoys for yet are shown as yellow markers.
[/list]
[b]Coordination[/b]
[list]
[*]Sharing puts the aircraft in one formation. Fixed-wing aircraft and helicopters can work one pattern together.
[*]Buoys are split by each aircraft's position, speed and buoy count, then rebalanced.
[*]Adding or removing an aircraft mid-pattern recomputes the layout and keeps buoys already placed.
[/list]
[b]Contact response[/b]
[list]
[*]Triggered by any submarine contact on your plot, hostile or unknown, once its position is known well enough to fix.
[*]The aircraft that can arrive soonest fixes it with a tight DICASS cluster or, for helicopters, a dipping sonar.
[*]Contacts are worked in order of threat to your ships.
[*]Weapons Free: a hostile contact with a good fix is attacked with a torpedo or depth charge. Weapons Tight: fix only. Weapons Hold: no response.
[*]The aircraft then resumes its interrupted pattern.
[/list]
[b]Convenience[/b]
[list]
[*]"ASW Pattern" is on the level-1 right-click menu, with "Enable / Disable ASW Prosecute".
[*]An "ASW" bottom-bar button: left-click repeats the last pattern, and the menu is available from there too.
[*]"Resume buoy deployment" restores a wiped queue.
[*]Map markers show queued buoys, and each ASW aircraft's label shows what it is doing.
[*]A detailed log (Sea Power\BepInEx\ASWAutoHunt.log) records every planned and dropped buoy, every detection and contact response, and every ASW weapon release.
[/list]
